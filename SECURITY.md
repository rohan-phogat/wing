# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report a security problem **privately**, not in a public issue:

**[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/rohan-phogat/wing/security/advisories/new)**

Say what you found, where (the Mac app, the Android app or Wingman's relay), how to reproduce it, and which versions you used. Screenshots or a short recording help.

While you look into it, please:

- use your own Mac and your own phone only, and do not touch anyone else's data or devices;
- give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you share it publicly.

## Which versions get fixes

The latest release. The Mac app updates itself, and the phone tells you when a newer Android app is ready, so everyone can be on it.

## How Wingman protects you

Everything between your phone and your Mac is sealed end to end, each new phone has to be approved on your Mac, and the app is protected by your PIN. What Wingman keeps, and what it cannot see, is on the [Privacy page](https://getwingman.tech/privacy).
