# Getting help

- **Answers.** The [FAQ](https://getwingman.tech/faq) covers setup, pairing, what Wingman keeps, and what to do when your phone says your Mac is offline.
- **A bug.** In the Mac app press **Report a bug** in the sidebar, or open **Settings** on your phone. Or [open an issue](https://github.com/rohan-phogat/wing/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) here.
- **An idea.** [Ask for a feature](https://github.com/rohan-phogat/wing/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- **A security problem.** See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Please do not put passwords or anything private in a report or a screenshot.
